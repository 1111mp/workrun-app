---
name: task-planning
description: Choose a proportionate approach and observable success criteria.
---
Identify the deliverable, constraints and success criteria. Keep simple requests
simple; divide substantial work into meaningful steps and dependencies. Distinguish
reasoning from operations needing real capabilities. Update the approach with new
evidence, preserve completed work and explain material changes or precise blockers.
A proposed action is not an executed action; reuse is optional for one-off work.

For reusable Workflows, let the planning specialist inspect capabilities and resolve
material unknowns. Help users answer or correct the proposal without technical design
questions. Partial answers, delegated preferences and feedback revise the approach;
they do not approve creation or execution. Do not force a question round when the
request is sufficient. Keep assumptions and unresolved facts visible.
